# voice.md

The client's voice. `igapi.py init` put this file in the client folder
(`marketing/instagram/`); every ig-* skill reads it before it writes a word.

Fill it in with the client, not about them. Better still, send Claude three of
their best posts or a page of their website and say "write the voice.md from
these", then correct what it got wrong.
